Shuo Zhou

Lecturer in Machine Learning, @The University of Sheffield.

I am a lecturer in machine learning and the PGR admission tutor at the School of Computer Science, University of Sheffield.

My current research focuses on robust, interpretable, and automated machine learning, with applications in biomedical and biological data analysis, particularly in neuroimaging and protein modelling. I am a co-creator and core developer of two open-source libraries, PyKale and KaleLinear, which provides a range of accessible multimodal and transfer learning algorithms.

I serve as a reviewer for the IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems (TNNLS), IEEE Transactions on Cognitive and Developmental Systems (TCDS), IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems & Rehabilitation Engineering (TNSRE), IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics (JBHI), Cerebral Cortex, Autism Research, European Child & Adolescent Psychiatry, and Frontiers in Neuroinformatics. Additionally, I served as a PC member for the International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI) in 2022, 2023, and 2024, as well as for the International Conference on Medical Image Computing and Computer Assisted Intervention (MICCAI) 2024, 2025, and 2026.
